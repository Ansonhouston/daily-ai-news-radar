## 🇭🇰 繁體中文版（copy 呢段）

啱啱出咗條新片，講我今年點樣用一條 AI 生產線做短片。

以前我裝咗好多 AI 工具，工具 A 出咗嘢要自己搬去工具 B，中間甩漏又係自己執。表面上請咗一班 AI 員工，實際上全公司最忙嗰個係我自己。

後來先發現，問題唔係 prompt 寫得唔夠好，而係我從來冇定義過：上一步做完，點交畀下一步。就好似連鎖店味道一致，靠嘅唔係天才大廚，係每道工序同驗收位都寫低咗。

所以我改成一個「總指揮」：拆步驟 → 派畀唔同 agent → 收貨 → 唔過標準就退返重做。同 2025 年比快咗接近 20 倍，出街條 Reel 喺 Facebook 跑到 6 萬幾 views。

但講清楚：呢個數只證明套系統生產得出一條片，唔代表令佢爆 —— 爆唔爆仲有演算法同時機。

最大感受：AI 做 execution，人做 judgment。揀方向、拒絕唔夠好嘅答案、最後拍板，仲係要人。

## 🇬🇧 English version（copy this）

Just posted a new video on how I run short-form video through an AI production line this year.

I used to have a stack of AI tools. Tool A produced something, I carried it into tool B by hand, and every gap in between was mine to patch. On paper I had a team of AI workers. In practice I was the busiest person in my own company.

The problem was never my prompts — I had just never defined what gets handed from one step to the next. Like a chain restaurant: every branch tastes the same because every step and every checkpoint is written down, not because every branch has a great chef.

So I rebuilt it around an orchestrator: break the work into steps, assign each to an agent, inspect the output, send it back if it doesn't pass. Same work as 2025, roughly 20x faster. The Reel it produced later hit 62K views on Facebook.

To be clear: that number only proves the system can produce a video, not that it made the video take off — algorithm and timing did a lot of that.

Biggest takeaway: AI does execution, humans do judgment. Picking the direction, rejecting the not-good-enough answer, making the final call — still us.
