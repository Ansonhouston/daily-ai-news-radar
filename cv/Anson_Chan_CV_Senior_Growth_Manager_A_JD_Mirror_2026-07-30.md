## SIGNATURE CASE STUDIES

### 1. Paid Acquisition Turnaround — ROAS 0.3x to 3x in 90 Days

**Context.** A fashion and lifestyle retail client was running Meta Ads at 0.3x ROAS. Budget was concentrated in warm retargeting and the creative pool had gone stale — the account was harvesting existing demand at a loss rather than acquiring customers.

**Action.** Diagnosed the failure as creative and funnel, not audience, and isolated it with single-variable tests. Rebuilt the full-funnel strategy and reallocated **70% of budget to cold-audience prospecting** for net-new acquisition, rebuilding the creative pool around journey-stage messaging instead of one offer angle. Rebuilt landing page messaging to match ad promise and close the conversion gap.

**Result.** ROAS scaled from **0.3x to 3x within 90 days** on a majority-prospecting split — materially harder than a retargeting-led lift, because the gain came from net-new customers rather than existing demand.

### 2. Multi-Million HKD Budget with Revenue Accountability — HK$300K Incremental GMV in 5 Months

**Context.** Baby Central, Hong Kong's #1 baby e-commerce platform, scaling across HK and SEA. Trust-sensitive category: credibility gates conversion and engagement metrics actively mislead.

**Action.** Owned channel performance and budget allocation for **HK$500K+/month** across Meta and Google (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, retargeting) — roughly HK$6M annually. Built GA4 and Meta Ads Manager dashboards specifically to tie spend decisions to revenue rather than engagement proxies, with UTM and pixel governance maintained as testing infrastructure. Scaled a 200+ KOL programme as a measurable acquisition channel.

**Result.** **HK$300K incremental GMV within 5 months**, consistent **3–7x ROAS** on HK$500K+/month spend, and 30% organic traffic growth through integrated SEO and paid social.

### 3. Lifecycle Retention Programme — 30,000 Subscribers

**Context.** Acquisition was performing, but repeat purchase was under-exploited — new customers were not being converted into returning ones, leaving the economics dependent on continuous paid spend.

**Action.** Owned the Klaviyo content-to-conversion system end to end for **30,000 subscribers**: 2x weekly campaigns plus four automated flows — welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back — with audience segmentation and A/B testing on subject lines and offers. Coordinated the handover so acquisition creative and retention messaging told one continuous story rather than two disconnected ones.

**Result.** A working repeat-purchase engine driving measurable returning revenue, reducing reliance on paid acquisition alone and lifting the value of every acquired customer.

### 4. Testing Throughput via AI Pipeline — +200% Conversion Rate

**Context.** Creative testing was throughput-limited to a handful of variants per cycle, producing slow, low-confidence learning and long periods running unvalidated creative.

**Action.** Built an AI-assisted variant production pipeline (Claude and ChatGPT for copy and angles; Midjourney and Ideogram for visuals) raising output to **10+ variants per cycle**, while holding single-variable testing discipline so increased throughput did not destroy attribution. Documented winners into a playbook with the structural reason they won.

**Result.** **+200% conversion rate** across Meta Ads and Instagram, peak campaigns at **10x+ ROAS**, and testing cycle time cut **~50%** — compounding learning velocity across the account.

### 5. AI Operational Efficiency Proven on Live Distribution

**Context.** Running a multi-platform content operation solo, with no team and no media budget — the hardest possible test of whether AI-driven workflows actually produce results or just output.

**Action.** Designed an end-to-end pipeline: custom Claude skills for scripting, briefs and competitor teardowns; Midjourney and Ideogram for visual production; an automated carousel generation system with image-matching logic and enforced design standards; and reusable templates with QC checklists governing the chain.

**Result.** Sustained weekly output across YouTube, Instagram and Facebook as one person — including a **76,000-view organic Facebook Reel from a 77-follower page (~990x follower count)** with zero paid support, and 84,000+ YouTube views across 57 videos. Scripting time cut ~60%.
