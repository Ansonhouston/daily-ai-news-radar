## HOW I ALLOCATE AND MEASURE

**Budget architecture.** 70/30 split between proven channels and active tests — enough allocation to learn meaningfully without putting account performance at risk. Pacing reviewed against efficiency targets, not spend targets.

**Cost accountability down the funnel.** Decisions read at the level where they affect revenue: creative engagement → click-through → conversion rate → cost efficiency → return on spend. Anything that wins upstream and fails downstream is retired.

**Single-variable testing.** One variable per test — creative angle, then messaging, then format or placement — so results are attributable rather than directional. AI-driven throughput sits inside this discipline; volume without attribution is noise.

**Cold versus warm discipline.** Prospecting is treated as the growth engine and retargeting as harvest. When an account is failing, the diagnosis starts with whether spend is manufacturing demand or just collecting it.

**Retention as an economics lever.** Acquisition cost is only defensible if repeat purchase follows, so lifecycle automation and segmentation are managed as part of the same P&L rather than a separate email function.

**Measurement infrastructure.** GA4 and Meta Ads Manager dashboards, UTM taxonomy and pixel governance maintained continuously, with monthly senior-stakeholder reporting built on a single source of truth.

## SIGNATURE CASE STUDIES

### 1. Failing Account to Profitable Acquisition — ROAS 0.3x to 3x in 90 Days

**Context.** A fashion and lifestyle retail client was running Meta Ads at **0.3x ROAS** — losing roughly three dollars for every one returned. Budget was concentrated in warm retargeting and the creative pool had gone stale, so spend was collecting existing demand at a loss rather than acquiring customers.

**Action.** Diagnosed the failure as creative and funnel rather than audience, isolating it with single-variable tests before touching budget. Then reallocated **70% of spend to cold-audience prospecting**, rebuilt the creative pool around journey-stage messaging instead of a single offer angle, and rebuilt landing page messaging to match ad promise and close the conversion gap.

**Result.** ROAS scaled from **0.3x to 3x within 90 days** — on a majority-prospecting split, which is materially harder than a retargeting-led lift because the return came from net-new customers rather than harvested demand.
