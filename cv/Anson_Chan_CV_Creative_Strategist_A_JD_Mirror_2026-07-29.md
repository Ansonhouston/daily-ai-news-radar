## CREATIVE TESTING & PRODUCTION METHODOLOGY

**Testing framework.** 70/30 budget split between proven creative and active tests. Single-variable sequencing — hook first, then messaging angle, then format — so every result is attributable. Winners are documented into a creative playbook rather than re-derived each cycle.

**Metric ladder.** Creative decisions are read down the funnel, not at the top of it: thumb-stop rate and hook rate → CTR → CVR → CPA → ROAS. Creative that wins on engagement but fails downstream is retired, not scaled.

**Production system.** Reusable prompt templates with conditional logic and QC checklists, an automated carousel generation pipeline with image-matching logic and design standards, and asset naming conventions with tagging and version control so a growing library stays searchable as volume scales.

**Research loop.** AI-assisted competitor and trend monitoring — YouTube outlier detection, Instagram viral post analysis, platform change tracking — feeding directly into the next testing cycle rather than sitting in a report.
