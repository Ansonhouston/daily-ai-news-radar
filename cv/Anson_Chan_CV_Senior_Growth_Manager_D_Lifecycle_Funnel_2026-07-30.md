## SIGNATURE CASE STUDIES

### 1. Funnel Diagnosis Over Bid Management — ROAS 0.3x to 3x in 90 Days

**Context.** A fashion and lifestyle retail client was running Meta Ads at **0.3x ROAS**. The instinct in that situation is to change bidding or audiences; the actual problem was further down — the creative pool had gone stale and the landing page did not deliver on what the ads promised, so traffic arrived and left.

**Action.** Ran single-variable tests to isolate where the funnel was leaking before touching budget. Rebuilt the creative pool around **journey-stage messaging** rather than a single offer angle, rebuilt landing page messaging and structure to match ad promise, and reallocated **70% of budget to cold-audience prospecting** so the account was manufacturing demand rather than harvesting it.

**Result.** ROAS scaled **0.3x to 3x within 90 days** on a majority-prospecting split — a harder result than a retargeting-led lift, and one that held because the conversion path itself was fixed rather than the traffic re-bought.

### 2. Lifecycle Retention Engine — 30,000 Subscribers

**Context.** Acquisition was performing but repeat purchase was under-exploited. Unit economics depended on continuous paid spend, and acquisition creative and email messaging were effectively telling customers two different stories.

**Action.** Owned the Klaviyo content-to-conversion system end to end for **30,000 subscribers**: 2x weekly campaigns plus four automated flows — **welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase and win-back** — with audience segmentation and A/B testing on subject lines and offers. Coordinated the acquisition-to-retention handover so paid creative and lifecycle messaging carried one continuous narrative from first impression through to reorder.

**Result.** A working repeat-purchase engine driving measurable returning revenue — reducing dependence on paid acquisition and lifting the value of every customer the paid programme brought in.

### 3. Three-Stage Funnel Architecture with Journey-Stage Messaging

**Context.** A multi-regional hair care brand was running one message to everyone, which meant cold audiences got conversion messaging and warm audiences got introductions — both mismatched to intent.

**Action.** Built AI-generated customer personas, then designed distinct creative and messaging for each funnel stage — **Awareness, Consideration, Conversion** — with the testing structured so each stage could be optimised independently rather than as one blended account.

**Result.** Testing cycles cut **50%**, with each stage measurable on its own terms instead of results being averaged into noise.

### 4. Website Revamp Alongside Live Paid Performance

**Context.** At Baby Central, paid traffic was landing on a site whose user flow was limiting conversion. Buying more traffic into a leaking funnel would have raised cost without raising revenue.

**Action.** Led the in-house design team (two designers) through a **full website design and revamp**, improving user flow and conversion path, while keeping the HK$500K+/month paid programme running and the EDM, social and offline event workload delivered by the same three-person team.

**Result.** The revamp shipped without paid performance dropping — **ROAS held at 3–7x throughout** — and the improved conversion path compounded the value of every subsequent dollar of media spend.

### 5. Multi-Million HKD Budget with Revenue Accountability

**Context.** Baby Central, Hong Kong's #1 baby e-commerce platform, scaling across HK and SEA in a trust-sensitive category where engagement metrics mislead and only revenue is reliable.

**Action.** Owned channel performance and budget allocation across Meta and Google — Search, Shopping, Performance Max, retargeting — at **HK$500K+ per month (≈HK$6M annually)**. Built GA4 and Meta Ads Manager dashboards to tie spend to revenue rather than engagement proxies, with UTM and pixel governance maintained continuously. Scaled a 200+ KOL programme as a measurable acquisition channel.

**Result.** Consistent **3–7x ROAS**, **HK$300K incremental GMV within 5 months**, and 30% organic traffic growth via integrated SEO and paid social.
