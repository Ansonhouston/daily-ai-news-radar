## SIGNATURE CASE STUDIES

### 1. Early Generative AI Adoption in Commercial Creative — 2022

**Context.** At Baby Central, creative testing throughput was the bottleneck on a HK$500K+/month paid programme. Generative AI was not yet standard practice in Hong Kong marketing teams.

**Action.** Integrated ChatGPT and Ideogram directly into the creative production workflow — copy variant generation and visual asset production — and rebuilt the testing process around the higher throughput this made possible, rather than bolting AI onto an unchanged process.

**Result.** A/B testing cycles cut by 50% and content production cost reduced materially, roughly two years before industry-wide adoption. The account sustained consistent 3–7x ROAS on HK$500K+/month spend throughout.

### 2. AI Pipeline Enabling Solo Multi-Platform Output — @aieasyjob

**Context.** Building a multi-platform content brand from zero with no team, no budget and no existing distribution — a workload that normally requires a small production unit.

**Action.** Designed an end-to-end AI pipeline: Claude for scripting and structural editing, Midjourney and Ideogram for visuals, Seedance 2.0 for AI video, CapCut for assembly, and reusable templates with QC checklists governing the whole chain. Built an automated carousel generation system with image-matching logic to remove the design bottleneck entirely.

**Result.** Sustained weekly output across YouTube long-form and Shorts, Instagram Reels and carousels, and Facebook Reels as a single operator — 57 YouTube videos and 84,000+ views, with 7 videos published in the trailing 30 days. Scripting time cut ~60%.

### 3. AI Variant Production Driving +200% Conversion Rate

**Context.** Client creative testing was limited to a handful of variants per cycle, producing slow, low-confidence learning and long periods running unvalidated creative.

**Action.** Built an AI-assisted variant production pipeline generating 10+ creative variants per cycle across copy and visual, while holding single-variable testing discipline so that increased throughput did not compromise attribution.

**Result.** +200% conversion rate across Meta Ads and Instagram, peak campaigns at 10x+ ROAS, and testing cycle time cut ~50% — compounding learning velocity across the account.

### 4. Organic Creative Outlier — 76,000 Views from 77 Followers

**Context.** @aieasyjob's Facebook page had 77 followers and a channel median around 360 views per Reel — effectively zero distribution advantage.

**Action.** Applied a hook structure refined through weekly AI-assisted testing: first-frame pattern-interrupt, tension-led opening beat, payoff timed against observed drop-off points. Generated and tested hook and opening-visual variants weekly against native retention and thumb-stop data.

**Result.** 76,000 organic views on a single Reel — **~210x channel median, ~990x follower count**, zero ad spend — with a second Reel at 11,000 views confirming the result was structural, not incidental.
