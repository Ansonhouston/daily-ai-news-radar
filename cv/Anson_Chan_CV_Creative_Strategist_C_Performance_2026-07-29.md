## CREATIVE TESTING METHODOLOGY

**Budget architecture.** 70/30 split between proven creative and active tests — enough allocation to learn meaningfully without putting account performance at risk.

**Single-variable sequencing.** Hook first, then messaging angle, then format. One variable per test, so results are attributable rather than directional. Higher AI-driven throughput is held inside this discipline; volume without attribution is noise.

**Metric ladder.** Thumb-stop rate and hook rate → CTR → CVR → CPA → ROAS. Every creative decision is read at the level where it actually affects revenue, not at the level that flatters the creative.

**Winner scaling and documentation.** Winners are written into a creative playbook with the structural reason they won — hook mechanic, tension placement, payoff timing — so patterns compound across accounts instead of being re-derived each cycle.

**Measurement infrastructure.** GA4 and Meta Ads Manager dashboards, UTM taxonomy and pixel governance maintained as a precondition of testing, not an afterthought — monthly reporting to senior stakeholders built on the same source of truth.

**Research loop.** AI-assisted outlier detection across YouTube and Instagram plus platform-change monitoring, feeding the next cycle's test hypotheses directly.

## SIGNATURE CASE STUDIES

### 1. Paid Creative Turnaround — ROAS 0.3x to 3x in 90 Days

**Context.** A fashion and lifestyle retail client was running Meta Ads at 0.3x ROAS. Budget was concentrated in warm retargeting, and the creative pool had gone stale — the account was harvesting existing demand at a loss.

**Action.** Diagnosed the failure as creative, not audience, and ran single-variable tests to isolate which hooks were breaking. Rebuilt the full-funnel creative strategy from scratch and reallocated **70% of budget to cold-audience prospecting** for net-new acquisition, rebuilding the creative pool around journey-stage messaging instead of a single offer angle.

**Result.** ROAS scaled from **0.3x to 3x within 90 days** on a majority-prospecting split — a materially harder result than a retargeting-led lift, because the gain came from net-new customers rather than existing demand.

### 2. Revenue-Attributed Creative at Scale — HK$300K Incremental GMV in 5 Months

**Context.** Baby Central, Hong Kong's #1 baby e-commerce platform, scaling across HK and SEA. Trust-sensitive category: creative credibility directly gates conversion, and vanity engagement metrics are actively misleading.

**Action.** Owned creative direction and performance for HK$500K+/month across Meta and Google (Search, Shopping, Performance Max, retargeting). Built GA4 and Meta Ads Manager dashboards specifically to tie creative decisions to revenue rather than engagement proxies, and scaled a 200+ KOL programme as a measurable UGC engine rather than a brand-awareness line item.

**Result.** **HK$300K incremental GMV within 5 months**, consistent 3–7x ROAS on HK$500K+/month spend, and 30% organic traffic growth through integrated SEO and paid social.

### 3. Testing Throughput — +200% Conversion Rate at 10+ Variants Per Cycle

**Context.** Client creative testing was throughput-limited to a handful of variants per cycle, producing slow, low-confidence learning and long stretches running unvalidated creative.

**Action.** Built an AI-assisted variant production pipeline (Claude, ChatGPT for copy and angles; Midjourney, Ideogram for visuals) raising output to 10+ variants per cycle — while holding single-variable discipline so throughput did not destroy attribution.

**Result.** **+200% conversion rate** across Meta Ads and Instagram, peak campaigns at **10x+ ROAS**, and testing cycle time cut ~50%, compounding learning velocity across the account.

### 4. Organic Creative Outlier — Method Validated Without Budget

**Context.** @aieasyjob's Facebook page: 77 followers, channel median around 360 views per Reel. No paid amplification and no distribution advantage — the hardest possible test of whether creative structure alone can earn reach.

**Action.** Applied the same hook discipline used on paid accounts — first-frame pattern-interrupt, tension-led opening beat, payoff timed against observed drop-off — iterating weekly against native retention and thumb-stop data.

**Result.** **76,000 organic views on a single Reel — ~210x channel median, ~990x follower count, zero ad spend.** A second Reel at 11,000 views confirmed the result was structural rather than incidental. Independent evidence that the performance gains above come from creative method, not media budget.
