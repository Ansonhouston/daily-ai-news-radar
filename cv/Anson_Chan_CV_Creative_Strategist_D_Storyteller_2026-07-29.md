**Context.** @aieasyjob's Facebook page had 77 followers and a channel median around 360 views per Reel. No paid amplification, no existing audience to seed reach, no algorithmic advantage. Anything that travelled had to travel on the strength of the story itself.

**Action.** Developed and refined a repeatable story architecture through weekly iteration: a first-frame visual pattern-interrupt, a tension-led opening beat that poses a question the viewer needs answered, and a payoff timed against the exact points where retention data showed people leaving. **Result.** **76,000 organic views on a single Reel — roughly 210x the channel median and 990x follower count, with zero ad spend.** A second Reel built on the same architecture reached 11,000 views, confirming the outcome was reproducible structure rather than a one-off. On a page with no distribution to lend, story construction was the only available lever.

**Action.** Built the channel's entire editorial craft around translation rather than explanation. Each piece opens on a recognisable human situation, not a tool name; the technical payload is introduced only once the viewer has a reason to want it; and every concept is anchored to a concrete outcome the viewer can picture in their own working life. Developed the "Working Dad" AI series to attach abstract capability to a specific lived context.

**Result.** 84,000+ YouTube views across 57 videos and a sustained weekly publishing cadence as a solo operator, with content consistently reaching audiences well beyond the subscriber base — the mark of stories that get shared rather than merely watched.

## STORY ARCHITECTURE — HOW THE WORK IS BUILT

**Hook.** First frame is a visual pattern-interrupt, not a title card. The opening beat poses a question the viewer needs resolved, framed in a situation they recognise before any product or tool is named.

**Tension.** A specific, concrete stake — usually a real cost of doing it the old way — rather than a generic benefit claim. Tension placement is tested, not assumed.

**Payoff.** Timed against observed drop-off points in retention data, so the resolution lands where attention is actually still present rather than where the script would naturally put it.

**Return.** A consistent point of view across platforms, so returning viewers accumulate trust rather than just consuming individual pieces — the mechanism that converts audience into clients.

**Iteration.** Every element above is treated as a testable variable, scored weekly against hook rate, thumb-stop rate and retention curves, with winners documented into a playbook so patterns compound.
