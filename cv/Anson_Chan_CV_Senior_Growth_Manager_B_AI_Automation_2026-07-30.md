## SIGNATURE CASE STUDIES

### 1. Early Generative AI Adoption in Commercial Marketing — 2022

**Context.** At Baby Central, creative testing throughput was the bottleneck on a HK$500K+/month paid programme. Generative AI was not yet standard practice in Hong Kong marketing teams and there was no internal precedent to copy.

**Action.** Integrated ChatGPT and Ideogram directly into the production workflow for copy variants and visual assets — then rebuilt the testing process around the higher throughput this unlocked, rather than bolting AI onto an unchanged process. Established output standards so speed did not cost quality.

**Result.** **A/B testing cycles cut 50%** and production cost reduced materially, roughly two years before industry-wide adoption. The account sustained consistent **3–7x ROAS** on HK$500K+/month throughout, and delivered HK$300K incremental GMV in 5 months.

### 2. Testing Throughput Without Losing Attribution — +200% Conversion Rate

**Context.** Client creative testing was limited to a handful of variants per cycle. Slow, low-confidence learning meant long periods running unvalidated creative — and the obvious fix, more variants, normally destroys attribution.

**Action.** Built an AI-assisted variant production pipeline raising output to **10+ variants per cycle** across copy and visual, while enforcing **single-variable sequencing** — hook, then messaging, then format — so every result remained attributable at volume. Winners documented into a playbook with the structural reason they won.

**Result.** **+200% conversion rate** across Meta Ads and Instagram, peak campaigns at **10x+ ROAS**, testing cycle time cut ~50%. Learning velocity compounded across the account instead of resetting each cycle.

### 3. AI Efficiency Proven Under the Hardest Conditions

**Context.** Running a multi-platform content operation solo — no team, no media budget, no existing audience. If AI-driven workflows only produce volume rather than results, this is where it shows.

**Action.** Designed an end-to-end pipeline: custom Claude skills for scripting, briefs and competitor teardowns; Midjourney and Ideogram for visuals; automated carousel generation with image-matching logic and enforced design standards; reusable templates with QC checklists governing the whole chain.

**Result.** Sustained weekly output across YouTube, Instagram and Facebook as one person — **76,000-view organic Facebook Reel from a 77-follower page (~990x follower count, ~210x channel median)** with zero paid support, a second Reel at 11,000 views confirming it was structural, and **84,000+ YouTube views across 57 videos**. Scripting time cut ~60%.

### 4. Paid Acquisition Turnaround — ROAS 0.3x to 3x in 90 Days

**Context.** A fashion and lifestyle retailer was running Meta Ads at 0.3x ROAS, with budget concentrated in warm retargeting and a stale creative pool — harvesting existing demand at a loss.

**Action.** Diagnosed the failure as creative and funnel rather than audience, isolating it with single-variable tests. Reallocated **70% of budget to cold-audience prospecting**, rebuilt the creative pool around journey-stage messaging using AI-generated variants, and rebuilt landing page messaging to match ad promise.

**Result.** ROAS **0.3x to 3x within 90 days** on a majority-prospecting split — a harder result than a retargeting-led lift, because the gain came from net-new customers.

### 5. Lifecycle Automation — 30,000 Subscribers

**Context.** Acquisition was working but repeat purchase was under-exploited, leaving unit economics dependent on continuous paid spend.

**Action.** Owned the Klaviyo system end to end: 2x weekly campaigns plus four automated flows — welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back — with segmentation and A/B testing, coordinated so acquisition and retention messaging told one continuous story.

**Result.** A working repeat-purchase engine driving measurable returning revenue and lifting the value of every acquired customer.
